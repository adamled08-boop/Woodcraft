Simple Garden Planter

A beginner-friendly wooden planter that can be built with basic tools.

Beginner Difficulty
2–3 hrs Build time
£25 Estimated cost

📐 Cut List

  • 2 × Side Boards
    600 mm × 250 mm × 18 mm
  • 2 × End Boards
    364 mm × 250 mm × 18 mm
  • 4 × Bottom Boards
    400 mm × 90 mm × 18 mm

🛠 Tools

  • 📏 Tape measure
  • ✏️ Pencil
  • ⬜ Square
  • 🪚 Saw
  • 🔩 Drill
  • 🔧 Screwdriver

🪵 Materials

  • 4 × timber boards
  • Exterior wood screws
  • Exterior wood treatment

STEP 1 OF 5

Measure & Mark

Measure your timber carefully and mark each cut using a pencil and square.

STEP 2 OF 5

Cut the Timber

Cut each piece along your marked lines. Check every measurement before making the cut.

STEP 3 OF 5

Build the Sides

Position the side pieces together. Pre-drill the screw holes and secure the pieces with exterior screws.

STEP 4 OF 5

Assemble the Planter

Join the remaining pieces together. Check that the planter is square before tightening all screws.

STEP 5 OF 5

Finish the Planter 🎉

Sand any rough edges and apply a suitable exterior wood treatment. Allow it to dry according to the manufacturer's instructions.
